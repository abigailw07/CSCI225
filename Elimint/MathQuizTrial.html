💡 ABOUT THE PROJECT

Big brains start with small steps.

Elimint is a web-based arithmetic quiz project designed to make math practice enjoyable for elementary school students.

Students can practice ten math topics, receive instant feedback, and use the Master Quiz to identify areas that may need more practice.
