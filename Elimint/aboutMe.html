About Elimint

Welcome to Elimint! Our goal is to make learning mathematics more enjoyable through interactive quizzes and engaging challenges.

Our Mission

Elimint was created to provide students with a fun and accessible way to practice mathematical concepts, improve problem-solving skills, and build confidence in mathematics.

What We Offer

Elimint includes individual quizzes covering different mathematical topics, along with a Master Quiz that combines questions from all topics into one challenge.

Meet the Team

Elimint was developed as a student project with the purpose of combining education and technology into an interactive learning experience.
